## Important before launch

**These are stock photos of other people's clinics, staff and patients.** They are
licensed and legal to use, but they are not this practice. For a real clinic site they
should be replaced with photography of the actual premises, team and equipment — local
patients notice, and stock imagery is the fastest way to make a real practice look
generic.

Two rules when swapping them in:

- Never present a stock model as a named member of staff. The team cards deliberately use
  initials rather than photos for this reason; only add a portrait once it is genuinely
  that practitioner.
- Never publish a patient's photo without written consent.

## What NOT to do

Don't take images from Google Images search results. Google is an index, not a licence —
almost everything there belongs to a stock agency, a photographer or another clinic, and
using it on a commercial site is copyright infringement. Use Pexels, Unsplash, a paid
stock licence, or your own photography.

See `README.md` in this folder for how to wire a file into a content record.
