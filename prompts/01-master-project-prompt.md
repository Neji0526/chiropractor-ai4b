# Chiropractor Lead Generation Website — Master Project Prompt

## Goal
Build a professional chiropractor website designed primarily to generate qualified patient leads, appointment requests, and phone calls.

## Stack
- Next.js 15 App Router
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- Supabase Auth
- Supabase Storage

## Design Direction
Use a clean, bright, trustworthy light theme. The site should look like a real local chiropractic practice, not an AI-generated SaaS landing page.

Avoid dark themes, neon colors, excessive gradients, generic AI copy, fake statistics, and unnecessary animations.

## Target Visitors
People looking for chiropractic care such as:
- Back pain care
- Neck pain care
- Chiropractic adjustments
- Sports injury care
- Posture support
- Headache-related chiropractic care
- Wellness chiropractic care

Do not make unsupported medical claims or promise guaranteed outcomes.

## Main Conversions
- Request an appointment
- Call the clinic
- Submit an inquiry

## Public Pages
- Home
- About
- Services
- Individual Service
- Chiropractors / Team
- Testimonials
- FAQ
- Blog / Resources
- Contact
- Request Appointment

## Homepage
Include:
- Clear hero with primary appointment CTA
- Phone CTA
- Trust section
- Conditions/services overview
- Why choose the clinic
- Chiropractor/team introduction
- What to expect
- Testimonials
- FAQ
- Location/contact section
- Final appointment CTA

## CMS
All niche-specific content should be editable from the admin panel:
- Services
- Conditions
- Practitioners
- Testimonials
- FAQs
- Blog posts
- Locations
- Business hours
- Homepage content
- Contact information
- SEO metadata

## Lead Management
Capture:
- Name
- Email
- Phone
- Preferred service
- Preferred contact method
- Message
- Source page
- Status
- Created date

Statuses:
- New
- Contacted
- Qualified
- Appointment Scheduled
- Closed

## SEO
Support:
- Dynamic metadata
- Canonical URLs
- Open Graph
- Sitemap
- robots.txt
- LocalBusiness/appropriate structured data
- FAQ structured data where appropriate
- Service/location landing pages

## Core Principle
Build around:
Visitor Intent → Trust → Relevant Service → Clear CTA → Lead Capture
