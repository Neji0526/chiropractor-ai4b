# Chiropractor Website — Lead Generation Prompt

Optimize the chiropractic website around qualified patient inquiries.

## Primary CTAs
- Request an Appointment
- Call Now
- Contact the Clinic

## Lead Form
Keep the initial form simple.

Recommended fields:
- Full name
- Phone
- Email
- Service/area of interest
- Preferred contact method
- Short message
- Consent acknowledgement when required

Do not ask for detailed medical histories in a general marketing lead form.

## CTA Placement
Place context-appropriate CTAs in:
- Header
- Hero
- Service pages
- Practitioner sections
- FAQ area
- Contact/location section
- Footer/final CTA

## Trust Elements
Use only verified clinic information:
- Practitioner credentials
- Real testimonials
- Clinic photos
- Location
- Business hours
- Contact details
- Clear explanation of what to expect

## Lead Tracking
Store:
- Source page
- UTM source
- UTM medium
- UTM campaign
- Created date
- Lead status

## Conversion Principles
Prioritize clarity over hype.

Each major page should answer:
- Is this service relevant to me?
- Why should I trust this clinic?
- What happens next?
- How do I contact them?

Do not use fake urgency, fake reviews, fabricated results, or guaranteed treatment outcomes.
