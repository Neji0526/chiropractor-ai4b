# Chiropractor Website — UI/UX Design Prompt

Design a conversion-focused website for a real local chiropractic practice.

## Direction
The site should feel:
- Bright
- Clean
- Calm
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Human
- Local

Use a light theme as the default.

## Visual System
Favor:
- White/off-white backgrounds
- Soft natural neutrals
- Restrained blue/green accents
- High-readable text
- Generous whitespace
- Realistic clinic/practitioner photography

Avoid:
- Dark themes
- Neon accents
- Heavy gradients
- Glassmorphism everywhere
- Futuristic AI imagery
- Generic SaaS cards
- Excessive animation

## Conversion UX
The user should quickly understand:
1. What the chiropractor offers
2. Whether the clinic may be relevant to their needs
3. Where the clinic is
4. How to contact the clinic

Use clear CTAs:
- Request an Appointment
- Call the Clinic
- Contact Us

## Homepage Flow
Hero → Trust → Services/Conditions → Practitioner → What to Expect → Testimonials → FAQ → Location → Final CTA.

## Mobile
Design mobile-first.

Keep appointment and phone actions easy to access without overwhelming the visitor.

## Content Style
Use natural, specific local-practice language.

Avoid robotic phrases such as:
- Unlock your potential
- Revolutionary solutions
- Transform your journey
- Cutting-edge wellness experience

Do not invent patient outcomes, awards, years of experience, review counts, or credentials.
